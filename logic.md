# Build a High-Fidelity Prototype for a Medical Exam Preparation Platform

Act as a Senior Product Designer, Senior UX Architect, and Senior Frontend Engineer.

Create a modern, premium, responsive web application prototype for a Medical Exam Preparation Platform.

This is NOT a generic Learning Management System. It is a specialized medical exam preparation platform similar to MRCEM Success, EMAM Academy, and Bromley Emergency, designed for doctors preparing for emergency medicine certification exams.

The prototype should look production-ready and suitable for investor demos, stakeholder reviews, and client presentations.

## Brand

Platform Name:
EM Hub

Alternative names that may appear in branding:

* EM Mentor
* EM Tutor
* EM Master

Use EM Hub as the primary brand.

Design style:

* Modern healthcare SaaS
* Clean and professional
* Premium medical education platform
* White, blue, and teal color palette
* Minimalistic UI
* Mobile responsive
* Accessibility compliant
