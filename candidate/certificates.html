Certificates are issued automatically once you complete 80% of a course. Each carries a unique number and a QR code linking to a public verification record, so it can be included in your training portfolio or ARCP evidence.

Verification

Anyone can verify an EM Hub certificate without an account.

  1. Scan the QR code on the certificate, or visit the verification page directly.
  2. Enter the certificate number printed at the foot of the document.
  3. The record returns the holder's name, course, completion date and issue status.

Verification records are permanent and are not affected by your subscription lapsing.
